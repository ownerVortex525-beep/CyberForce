Acceptance Rules & Disclaimer

CyberForce Case Report System use karne ka matlab hai ki aap in sabhi rules se sehmat hain.

✅

1. Sachhi Jankari

Report mein sirf satya jankari bharein. Jhoothi complaint ya galat saboot dena ek dandaniya apradh hai.

⚖️

2. Legal & Ethical Use

Yeh report sirf law enforcement / cyber cell mein submission ke liye hai. Harassment, doxxing, badla ya kisi ko badnam karne ke liye iska use varjit hai.

🔒

3. Privacy First

Aapka sab data aapke device par hi rehta hai — hum kuch bhi upload ya store nahi karte. Report kiske sath share karni hai, iski zimmedari user ki hai.

🧾

4. Evidence Integrity

Screenshots tamper na karein. SHA-256 hashes evidence ki authenticity prove karne ke liye hain — inhe badalna report ko invalid karta hai.

🚨

5. Official Channels

Financial fraud hone par turant National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 par call karein. Yeh system police/cyber cell ka substitute nahi, sirf madad hai.

🤝

6. User Responsibility

System ka durupayog karne par kanooni karyawahi ki zimmedari poori tarah user par hogi. CyberForce ka mission hai: Learn. Protect. Report.

Declaration of Use

Is system ko use karke main pushti karta/karti hoon ki main yeh reports keval sachhi jankari ke sath, kanooni karwai hetu, aur ethical tareeke se use karunga/karungi. National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
