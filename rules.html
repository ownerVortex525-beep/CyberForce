Acceptance Rules & Disclaimer

Read these rules before using the CyberForce Case Report System. Using the system means you accept all of them.

✅

1. Truthful Information

Provide only true information. Filing a false complaint or fabricating evidence is a punishable offence.

⚖️

2. Legal & Ethical Use

Reports are intended solely for submission to law enforcement / cyber cell. Use for harassment, doxxing, revenge or defamation is strictly prohibited.

🔒

3. Privacy First

All data stays on your device — nothing is uploaded or stored by us. Sharing the report is the user's responsibility.

🧾

4. Evidence Integrity

Do not tamper with screenshots. SHA-256 hashes prove evidence authenticity — altering them invalidates the report.

🚨

5. Official Channels

In case of financial fraud, call the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 immediately. This system assists — it does not replace — the police / cyber cell.

🤝

6. User Responsibility

Legal liability for any misuse lies entirely with the user. CyberForce mission: Learn. Protect. Report.
